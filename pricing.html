Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel anytime from your account settings. You keep Pro features until your billing period ends.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit/debit cards and PayPal through Stripe.
What happens to my data if I downgrade?
Your instances, mods, and settings stay. Pro-only features (AI, Quick Servers) become unavailable until you re-subscribe.
Is there a free trial for Pro?
Not currently — but the Free tier is fully functional. You can upgrade anytime to unlock Pro features.
