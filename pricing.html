Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Your Pro features will remain active until the end of your current billing period. No questions asked, no hidden fees.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) as well as PayPal. All payments are securely processed through Stripe.

Do I keep my worlds if I downgrade?

Absolutely. Your worlds, instances, and mods are always yours. Downgrading only affects access to Pro features like unlimited Loomie AI and Quick Servers. Your data is never deleted.

Is there a free trial?

The Free tier is available forever with no time limit. You can use Loom with all core features at no cost. Pro and Pro Max offer additional capabilities for players who want more power and flexibility.
