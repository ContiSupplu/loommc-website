What sets Loom apart

Loomie AI

Your game crashes. Instead of digging through log files or asking strangers on Discord, Loomie figures out which mod broke, removes or replaces it, and gets you back in the game. You can also ask it to install mods, change your settings, or answer any Minecraft question — by typing, talking, or sharing a screenshot.

Dynamic Island

A small floating bar that lives at the top of your screen — even while you're playing. It shows your download progress, what song is playing on Spotify, and whether your server is online. When something goes wrong, it pops up with the error and a one-click fix. When nothing is happening, it stays out of your way.

Java + Bedrock

Play both editions from the same app. Browse your Bedrock worlds, install addons, block Marketplace ads, and launch — without ever opening a second launcher. No other third-party launcher supports both.
