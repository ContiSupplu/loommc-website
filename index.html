What you get

One-click instances

Pick a version and loader. Loom handles Java, libraries, and assets. Your instance is ready in seconds.

Mod management

Browse and install from Modrinth. Drag-and-drop JARs. Automatic compatibility checks.

Fast launches

Parallel downloads, background caching, pre-fetched manifests. No waiting around.

Discord presence

Shows your instance, game time, and version on your Discord profile.

Skin browser

Look up players, preview skins in 3D, and manage your friends list.

Clean interface

Dark glass-effect design inspired by macOS. No ads, no clutter, no noise.

Dynamic Island

A HUD that renders directly inside Minecraft as a Fabric mod. It sits at the top of your screen and changes based on what you're doing — without blocking gameplay.

Available on 1.21.1 and 1.21.11 with Fabric. Installs automatically.

Spotify controls

Current track, artist, album art. Skip, pause, and adjust volume from the island.

Waypoint navigation

Live compass with distance counting down as you walk toward your target.

Combat tracker

Auto-detects PvP. Shows opponent health, armor, and weapon in real-time.

Timers and alerts

Countdown timers, weather alerts, low-health warnings, and biome notifications.

Five themes

Midnight, Neon, Ember, Arctic, and Album Art. Switch with a keybind.

Loomie

An AI assistant built into the launcher and accessible from the Dynamic Island. Powered by Gemini, trained on everything Minecraft.

Full Minecraft knowledge

Crafting recipes, enchantment tables, brewing charts, mob stats, biome details, redstone mechanics. Ask in plain English.

Conversational

“What does Smite V do?” or “How do I find a nether fortress?” — Loomie answers naturally.

Available in-game

Open Loomie from the Dynamic Island while playing. No alt-tabbing. Answers appear on your screen.
