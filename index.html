Features

Loom packs a full toolkit into one clean launcher. From AI-powered help to one-click servers, it all just works.

Smart Library

Create and manage instances, mods, and versions from a single unified library. Everything organized, always accessible.

Loomie AI

Your Gemini-powered companion. Get instant help with crashes, mod conflicts, performance tuning, and setup guidance.

Dynamic Island

An Apple-style HUD overlay that shows real-time launch progress, download status, and game state at a glance.

Bedrock Edition

Launch and manage Bedrock Edition directly. Browse add-ons, configure settings, and play with friends across platforms.

Quick Servers Coming Soon

One-click server hosting built right into the launcher. Create, configure, and share servers with zero friction.

Performance

Optimized JVM flags, automatic performance mods, and Cache & Skip for faster load times. Every frame counts.

Gallery

Record gameplay and capture screenshots without leaving the launcher. Your best moments, saved and organized.

Browse & Install

Search Modrinth, CurseForge, and more. Find mods, modpacks, shaders, and resource packs in one unified browser.
